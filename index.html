09Questions

Questions leaders often ask

Still unsure? Ask us directly.

01We’re not technical. Is this for us?

Yes. We work with business leaders and their teams, not engineers. Most teams start with training and build from there.

02How is training different from strategy?

AI training gets you and your team using AI well: team training, leadership workshops and teaching bots. AI strategy is guidance for integrating AI into your business through your leaders; our consulting sits inside it.

03Do we need a strategy before training?

No. Many teams train first, and the strategy gets clearer once people use AI every day. The readiness check suggests a starting point.

04What happens to our information?

We agree what can and can’t go into AI tools before any work starts, and teaching bots are built only on knowledge you approve.

05What does it cost?

It depends on the size of your team and the scope. Drop us a line and we’ll come back with a clear quote.
