02Font licence

F37 Aria Regular, for the wordmark

F37 prices each licence per style and by company size, and charges once. Which licence you need depends on what the font is used for: a logo needs the Logo licence.

About the £40 in the cart

The cart shows the Basic licence, which doesn't cover the wordmark. F37's Basic EULA says you may not “create logos, trade marks or other service marks using the Font (a separate licence is available for this from our website(s))” (clause 1.7.9), and its introduction says it is not for logo use. Turning the type into an image doesn't change that: what matters is that it's used as a logo. The wordmark needs the Logo licence, £400 at the Startup tier. The Basic licence is only worth adding if the team also wants Aria for documents or presentations.

05Notes

Assumptions and caveats

  • The Logo licence covers one version of one logo. A second lockup (for example a stacked version) may need a second licence, and if the wordmark's letters have been altered from the font, F37 has to approve it. Worth confirming with F37 before buying.
  • F37 prices by employees, including contractors across the group. Declaring too few costs a £300 admin fee plus the difference.
  • Prices exclude GST and VAT. F37 calculates tax at checkout, and overseas purchases may carry card fees.
  • AUD figures are converted at the rates above and rounded; recheck before buying, as rates and promotions change.
  • Package ratings are our assessment for this site from each provider's published features, not independent benchmarks. None of the free plans include an uptime guarantee (SLA).
  • Netlify's free plan isn't offered as a package: each update uses credits, and the site is paused once they run out.
  • Neither video avatar captures leads or hands over to a person on its own; on this site they would sit alongside the contact form and readiness check. A cheaper way to get the video-bubble look without AI is a pre-recorded intro (for example VideoAsk) that opens a chat tool such as Chatbase.
  • Assistant prices are the vendors' published plans. Usage above each plan's allowance is charged extra, so a busy month costs more.
