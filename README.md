# Actualise AI — site concept

A design concept for the new actualiseai.com.au, with the proposed brand identity. It is not the live site.

**Live:** https://takatime93.github.io/actualiseai/

## Pages

- `/` — the homepage, using the current site's copy. It opens on a live 3D city of cubes; the intro has a street-level view driving the city's main avenue.
- `/identity/` — the Actus brand identity: idea, symbol, logo rules, colour concepts, type, graphic language, motion, examples in use and next steps.
- `/lab/city/` and `/lab/word/` — test pages for the city and for the earlier 3D wordmark. Not in the menu.

## Using it

- The header switches between two colour concepts (Ink and Cobalt) and dark or light mode. Dark is the default. The choice carries across pages.
- The opening sequence (the AI mark, then the wordmark) plays once per visit. Click or press Esc to skip.
- Forms are not connected: submitting shows a note and sends nothing.
- With "reduce motion" turned on in system settings, the page is shown still.

## Notes

- Photography is from Unsplash (free licence) as placeholders for Actualise's own.
- The wordmark is set in F37 Aria; a Logo Licence from F37 is needed before public use.
- Search engines are asked not to index this concept.

## Editing

Everything here is generated by `source/site.py` (with `city.js`, `home.js` and `hero3d.js`) in the identity project. Edit those, rebuild, then commit and push from this folder.
